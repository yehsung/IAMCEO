# DRAFT — IAMCEO Contributor License Agreement (not active)

> **Status: draft only. Not in force.**
> - **When it would apply.** Only to contributions submitted after the maintainer announces it and the contributor explicitly agrees. It does not apply retroactively to any earlier contribution.
> - **Before activation.** Have it reviewed by a lawyer.

## Purpose and effect on contributors (plain summary)

- **Purpose.** The maintainer may later need to do two things for the whole codebase: grant someone separate commercial permission, or change the license of future versions. Contributors' rights otherwise stay the same.
- **No assignment.** You keep your copyright. You do not assign it.
- **Non-exclusive licence.** You grant the maintainer a non-exclusive licence that also allows relicensing and sublicensing your contribution. You can still license your own contribution to anyone else.
- **Your contribution stays usable.** It remains available to the public under the project's license that applied when you contributed. A later relicence does not remove the copies people already received.
- **If you decline.** Declining means the maintainer will not merge your contribution if the CLA is active. It changes nothing else.
